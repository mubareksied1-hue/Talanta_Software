My Favorite Recipe: Pasta Carbonara

Ingredients:

  1. Spaghetti
  2. Eggs
  3. Pancetta
  4. Parmesan Cheese
  5. Black Pepper

Cooking Time:

  • Preparation Time: 10 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 20 minutes
  • Total Time: 30 minutes
  • Temperature: 350°F
  • Servings: 4
  1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together eggs and grated Parmesan cheese.
  3. Cook pancetta in a pan until crispy, then remove from heat.
  4. Drain the spaghetti and return it to the pot. Add the pancetta and egg mixture, tossing quickly to coat the pasta without scrambling the eggs.
  5. Season with black pepper and serve immediately.
