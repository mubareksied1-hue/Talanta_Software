ABOUT US

The restaurants at Sarova Hotels are designed to offer guests a memorable culinary journey that blends Kenyan tradition with international flavors. At Sarova Stanley, guests can enjoy the historic Thorn Tree Café, famous for its open-air courtyard and traveler’s message board, alongside the award-winning Thai Chi Restaurant, which serves authentic Thai cuisine. The Exchange Bar adds a touch of heritage with cocktails and light meals in a classic setting that reflects Nairobi’s rich history.
At Sarova Panafric, the Flame Tree Restaurant stands out with its garden views and diverse menu of African and international dishes, making it a favorite for Sunday brunches and themed buffets. The Pool Deck Bar provides a relaxed atmosphere with fresh juices and cocktails, while Tulia Wellness Café caters to health-conscious diners with smoothies, salads, and herbal teas. Each venue emphasizes fresh, locally sourced ingredients and a welcoming ambiance.
Nairobi, Sarova’s other properties also feature distinctive dining experiences. At Sarova Whitesands in Mombasa, guests enjoy beachfront seafood grills and casual cafés, while Sarova Mara Game Camp offers bush dinners under the stars with Maasai-inspired menus. Sarova Lion Hill in Nakuru provides panoramic lake-view dining at the Flamingo Restaurant. Across all locations, Sarova restaurants combine culture, hospitality, and flavor, ensuring that dining is not just a meal but an integral part of the overall guest experience.
